What is this paper about?

The pebble motion problem starts with labeled pebbles placed on the vertices of a finite undirected simple graph and asks for a sequence of legal moves from one configuration to another. A legal move transfers one pebble to an adjacent vertex that is currently unoccupied, so collisions are avoided by construction. The problem is a basic graph-reconfiguration model, closely related to the fifteen puzzle and to multi-agent path finding. Its central complexity question is not only whether a target configuration is reachable, but also how many moves a shortest solution may require as a function of the board structure.
