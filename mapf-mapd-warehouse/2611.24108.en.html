Multi-Agent Path FindingMAPF
In short: In many deployed mobile robot systems, however, not every robot is assigned a service task in every planning episode. Some robots must travel from their starts to designated targets to perform transportation, pick-and-place, or laboratory operations, while others have no task targets but still occupy the shared workspace. We established an asymmetric decomposition and proposed FOCBS, which searches over assigned agents with CBS, routes unassigned agents with a min-cost flow oracle, and converts routing failures into assigned-agent constraints through flow-conflict branching.
